Geevheit Intelligence 360° is NOT an ERP.

It is NOT a Fleet Management System.

It is NOT a TMS.

It is an Enterprise Transportation Intelligence Platform.

The platform transforms operational events into business intelligence.

The platform exists to reduce operational costs.

Every module must contribute to this objective.

# PRODUCT MISSION

Transform operational data into intelligent business decisions.

Every feature must answer at least one of these questions:

• Where am I losing money?

• What should I do today?

• Which asset deserves attention?

• Which maintenance should be anticipated?

• Which driver should receive training?

• Which vehicle should be replaced?

• Which tire is increasing operational costs?

• Which workshop is the most efficient?

• What is the profitability of each vehicle?
